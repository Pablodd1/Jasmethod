# Sport records, HYROX and HR references

The profile's previous personal-record form requested a generic numeric value and defaulted to seconds. Activity rows labeled as best times displayed pace or speed, omitted source/date and hid results after the first six. The reader also used planned duration even where an explicit actual duration existed.

The replacement offers an expandable sport/result form: running, cycling, swimming, triathlon, HYROX and strength; total time, pace, speed, average power, distance, load and repetitions. Clock input accepts minutes, m:ss or h:mm:ss. Distances and units remain explicit. The observed date is required. The server repeats typed conversion and compares it with the submitted record before saving. Duplicate typed additions are rejected; reviewed revision, conflict handling and original manual records remain protected. Legacy records remain readable in their stored units. No result automatically becomes FTP, CSS, threshold pace or measured capacity.

Recorded activity results use explicit actual duration or completed unplanned duration. They retain source and observation timestamp/local date, display the actual recorded minutes, and are not certified race/segment PRs. Matching distance is still the existing whole-activity window of target distance through +1%; times are not extrapolated to an exact distance. Missing actual duration remains unavailable. Activity records are not rewritten.

HYROX already has a private, revision-checked, explicitly confirmed result log. It stores eight running splits, eight station splits and separate Roxzone/transition time, event/date and Singles Open/Pro division. Profile now loads a read-only summary from that existing owner-only endpoint on expansion. Partial results have no complete finish time. Read failures and unreadable records do not become an empty history. The existing full HYROX screen remains the editor. A generic device activity does not establish station splits, official result verification, doubles or relay division support. This change adds no automatic official-results integration, station-load inference, provider request or external-AI processing.

HR display uses the saved maximum-HR reference and the existing five-zone calculation. Cycling's fixed -6 bpm adjustment is a template, not a measured individual offset. The display now distinguishes that calculation from a watch's configured zones, labels source/date as unverified and labels the HR-ratio VO2 estimate as unmeasured. No numerical HR reference or zone formula was silently changed. Comparing an individual athlete's zone settings still requires their actual dated source and measurement method; a workout peak alone cannot establish physiological maximum HR. HYROX station-specific zones are not verified here.

Verification covers clock conversion, invalid/future dates and units, actual versus planned duration, ownership, reviewed revisions, tampered converted records, duplicates, save failure/retry, and incomplete/error HYROX summaries. The EN/ES mobile database/browser journey saves/reloads a clock result and opens HYROX history. These are engineering checks; provider transport and physiological accuracy are separate acceptance requirements.
