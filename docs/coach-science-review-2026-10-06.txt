JMM — coaching reliability and science review, 6 October 2026

Release scope
This change improves access and error handling for J Koach, corrects specific training/fueling defects, expands the public science explanation, and replaces contradictory release instructions. It is not evidence that every application workflow or provider integration is ready.

J Koach
The user reports chat will not open/load. Production conversation tables and their migrations were verified present through a read-only schema check. The available browser was signed out; the original authenticated failure has not been reproduced. A query for recent production HTTP500 logs returned no entries, which does not rule out browser, authentication or network failures.
Added /coach with protected access, a route error boundary and direct desktop/mobile links. Existing Today chat remains compatible. Session verification has a15-second timeout and explicit retry state rather than endless loading or an unexplained homepage redirect. Chat handles expired sessions and non-JSON gateway errors without displaying parser/internal response details. No migration, provider credential change or athlete-history rewrite is part of this release.

Training corrections
The easy-training preference no longer promotes easy work or expands duration solely to hit a percentage. It remains a planning approximation based on session labels, not measured time in physiological zones, and is not uniformly applied to every sport generator.
New HYROX weeks are bounded by allocated weekly minutes. Hardcoded long sets and compulsory race loads/depth jumps were replaced with reviewed within-budget instructions. Women Pro and Men Pro references and wall-ball target heights are distinct. Generic running, cycling and swimming base substitutions now encode easy title/type/zone consistently, rather than describing easy work while storing z5.
These are template corrections; they do not establish that generated sessions were tested as complete human interventions. Existing stored plans are not rewritten automatically.

Fueling and recovery
All rates mean total carbohydrate from food, drinks and gels combined. A universal minimum60g glucose/hour after one hour is not supported. General30–60g/hour and longer-session practiced60–90 guidance is differentiated from selected higher-intake research. Optional80/90/100 rehearsal targets require context and tolerance; they are not automatic weekly increases.
Corrected displayed carbohydrate:protein ratio to match the actual quantities. Ordinary recovery meal examples are distinguished from hourly carbohydrate restoration for a known short turnaround. Removed public instructions that treated thirst as a mandatory fluid minimum or automatically prescribed caffeine.
IMPORTANT: new detailed carbohydratePractice/nextSessionInHours engine inputs have tests but are not yet persisted or wired through the complete UI/API workflow. No claim of fully automated high-intake personalization is made.
