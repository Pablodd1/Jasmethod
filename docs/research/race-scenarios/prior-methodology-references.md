# Prior methodology references retained for review

These references were previously displayed in the public race explanation. The public page now focuses on neutral methodology and limitations; scholarly provenance remains in this repository. The items below retain prior research context and are not a new citation-quality or model-validation claim. For the sources checked for this implementation, use `model-evidence.md`.

- Running durability and marathon performance: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35511416/ . The large observational population does not validate JMM prediction accuracy.
- Stroke-specific critical speed and test reliability: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10875687/ . Reliability is not the same as open-water prediction accuracy.
- Road-cycling mechanical power model: DOI https://doi.org/10.1123/jab.14.3.276 . Measured mechanical parameters do not establish sustainable race power from FTP alone. The implementation research uses the DOI rather than relying on the previously displayed PubMed identifier.
- Drafting versus non-drafting triathlon predictors: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12904633/ . Small elite-athlete evidence should not be extrapolated across distance and ability.
- Simulated HYROX competition demands: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40230601/ . Early performance-determinant research does not validate a JMM scenario total.
