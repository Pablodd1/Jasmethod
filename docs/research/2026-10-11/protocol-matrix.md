## Reading the matrix

Evidence about a general training principle is not validation of every repetition, rest or progression. Study doses describe research, not automatic safe starting prescriptions. Numeric targets require the original sport-specific anchor; PPO, FTP, HRmax, CSS and lactate thresholds are not interchangeable. Exact full-dose trial replication is not claimed for app adaptations.

Adult status, sport-specific training history, injury/illness/symptoms, goal, existing load, safe equipment/setting, adequate time and recovery. Unknown data fails closed. These are product/coaching review gates, not medical clearance or validated injury prediction.

Unless explicitly study-described, progression/recovery rules here are JMM coaching inferences. Replace existing quality exposure instead of stacking all useful protocols. Preserve complete work/rest and truthful totals. No automatic increase from ambition or expert branding.

These studies do not supply a comprehensive contraindication list. Pain, unusual symptoms, illness, or relevant medical restrictions require stopping/review; chest pain, fainting or severe breathing difficulty warrant urgent medical attention. No personalized treatment advice is inferred.
