## Retrieval and adjudication

- Helgerud 2007: original PDF methods recovered through an institutional host and independently checked. All arms used a 5.3% treadmill incline; shortened summaries must not imply an exact flat-road replication.
- Seiler 2013: official publisher abstract plus original author-uploaded paper. Search extraction of the methods resolved the distinct recovery durations; the PDF’s normal text extraction was sparse. Original methods, not third-party workout websites, supply the numbers.
- Rønnestad 2020: complete official publisher methods were accessible despite blank PubMed retrieval. Counterbalanced allocation is not automatically randomized allocation. Warm-up included submaximal openers; any JMM modification must be explicit.
- Little 2010: original author-institution abstract verifies participants and main set. It does not verify every complete-session component. JMM warm-up/cool-down and effort cues need their own provenance.
- Støren 2008: original journal PDF mirrored by an academic site verifies interset rests and study progression; a mirror’s host does not convert the paper into secondary commentary.
- CSS and taper rows retain the earlier dossier’s verified extraction where fresh full-text retrieval did not succeed. Their entries are frameworks/measurement context, not newly verified executable sessions.
- Official Huberman/Galpin transcripts are educational sources and carry error warnings. Speaker-label inconsistencies reinforce the need to cite the discussion/chapter rather than treat every transcript label as unquestionable authorship.
- The 30 source-verified main-set rows span 17 source families, with multiple genuinely different arms in some studies. They are not 30 independent trials. Some are acute comparisons, some intervention trials, and some null or unfavorable results; main-set verification is not an endorsement.
