## Working workflow and architecture

The existing `/races/scenarios` workspace now begins with a compact EN/ES event/conditions card. Authenticated users explicitly search public RunSignup events by name or city, confirm an edition/division, confirm a registry address against US Census address matches, and retrieve conditions. Missing time/date/discipline remain missing. Civil timezone offsets are resolved automatically only when unambiguous; DST gaps/folds require correction or an explicit offset. Provider updates fill empty fields only and retain athlete corrections. Race changes are scenario drafts until the ordinary save action.

RunSignup was verified live on October 21: public Miami search returned 22 event/division candidates; the first registry address yielded one Census match. A registry address can be an organizer address and is never automatically certified as the start line. This is a bounded public catalog, not a worldwide official-event search engine. Arbitrary event websites are retained as references and never fetched. URL-only lookup, unsupported catalogs, non-US address resolution, missing coordinates, course FIT decoding and automatic triathlon course/transition extraction remain limitations. Existing reviewed GPX/manual course inputs and sport-specific dated anchors are reused. Unknown HYROX division/indoor conditions and marine conditions do not produce predictions.

New routes `/api/race-scenarios/lookup` and `/api/race-scenarios/environment` reuse `trainingAccess`, same-origin validation, bounded JSON, actor rate limits and private/no-store responses. Auth, unauthorized athlete targeting, invalid origin and injected health fields are rejected before provider calls. Endpoints are fixed HTTPS hosts, redirects refused, responses bounded and timeouts enforced. No arbitrary URL fetch, paid AI call, subscription, provider grant, athlete-data disclosure or schema/dependency change is introduced.

The weather orchestration reuses MET Locationforecast and adds NASA POWER hourly archive reads. Normalized SI snapshots retain actual sample time, event IANA timezone, requested/provider coordinates, retrieval/source-generation timestamps, source/model identity, nullable variables, dew-point derivation and explicit unavailable WBGT. Hourly POWER pressure converts kPa→hPa. Hourly Wh/m² over one hour is mean W/m²; precipitation is the hourly accumulation. Sea-level pressure is not substituted for surface pressure. Grid wind is not rider-relative airflow or course headwind.

Confirmed weather context can be attached to the existing scenario metadata and saved/reloaded through its owner-scoped, revision-checked immutable audit snapshot. Original context, joint seasonal samples and a separate manual copy are retained. These are reviewed client copies, not cryptographic/server certification of provider provenance. Attaching a point supplies labeled reviewed weather assumptions, without changing selected pace/power, workout approval or the saved calendar. Seasonal marginal medians are never assembled into one effective weather day by this new path. Existing daily summaries remain explicitly daily context, not hourly forecasts.

## Date selection, caching and operations

| Requested event time | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Past | NASA POWER UTC hourly grid estimates for the actual date; nearest sample within one hour; otherwise unavailable. Not instrument observations. |
| Future, inside returned MET coverage | Actual nearest forecast sample and generation/retrieval timestamps; unsupported values stay null. Stale fallback is not promoted to a fresh result. |
| Future, outside returned MET coverage | Five prior complete-year seasonal windows, ±7 local calendar days, matched civil start hour. Leap days center on February 28 in non-leap years. New-Year windows include the adjacent calendar year. |
| Insufficient archive | Abstain unless at least 3 seasonal years and 30 complete joint temperature/RH/wind samples exist. This is an operational coverage rule, not statistical validation. |
| Provider failure | Explicit unavailable context, never current weather presented as historical or historical data presented as a forecast. |

Seasonal results retain complete joint samples and separate median/p10/p90 descriptive ranges. No synthetic typical day combines unrelated marginal extremes. The five-year baseline is a recent seasonal reference, not a 30-year climate normal or a climate projection. Source sets are recorded per sample; source/model revisions and grid biases limit comparability. Daylight-saving offsets are applied per historical year; duplicate civil hours are not overweighted. Fractional-hour starts use the corresponding hourly bin, with actual UTC sample disclosed.

MET uses identifying server-side User-Agent, four coordinate decimals, in-flight coalescing, bounded cache, Expires and conditional Last-Modified revalidation. HTTP 429 honors Retry-After with a conservative minimum delay. POWER uses bounded 17-day UTC requests, a day-long in-memory cache, coalescing, at most five archive calls and an overall request budget; missing years remain missing. No scheduled poll or immediate retry is introduced. Process-local caches/budgets are not distributed quotas: a shared application cache/limit is needed before significant horizontal scale. Existing API logs must be used to review errors/fallbacks; no new persistent telemetry or monitoring service is claimed.

Set `RACE_ENVIRONMENT_ENABLED=false` or `RACE_PUBLIC_LOOKUP_ENABLED=false` to stop their respective new lookups. MET supports `RACE_WEATHER_USER_AGENT` with a real application contact. RunSignup caller token/secret remain server-only. RunSignup announced mandatory free caller registration from January 1, 2027; lookup refuses unregistered new calls from that date. Do not print caller secrets or provider URLs containing caller tokens.

## Scientific interpretation

Magnus liquid-water approximation: `e = 0.61094 * exp(17.625 * Td / (Td + 243.04))` kPa, and its inverse from temperature/RH. The supported implementation domain is −40 to 50°C, RH >0..100%; outside-domain moisture is unavailable. This is an approximation, not a humidity instrument calibration. Below-freezing phase convention matters: POWER's dew/frost point is not blindly mixed with the liquid-water convention, so dew point is derived consistently from validated T/RH. Tests cover saturation, zero humidity, known reference values, nonfinite values, implausible supersaturation and inconsistent overrides. Output precision is a display choice, not a scientific accuracy claim. Actual vapor pressure is not vapor-pressure deficit.

| Evidence reviewed | Population/protocol/outcome | Implication and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jenkins et al., 2023, DOI 10.1113/EP090969](https://physoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1113/EP090969) | Randomized crossover, 14 trained participants, 7 female; 45 min cycling then 20 km TT. Compared 18/27/36°C at 1.96 kPa and 36°C at 3.92 kPa, approximately 4.5 m/s airflow. Hot TT was 3.6–6% slower; added humidity impaired TT by 3.4% with reported uncertainty. | Supports separating moisture from temperature and considering airflow. These group cycling effects are not individual running/triathlon/HYROX constants. No coefficient is copied into the engine. |
| [Ely et al., 2007, DOI 10.1249/mss.0b013e31802d3aba](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17473775/) | Observational marathon finishing times and weather, with performance groups. | Population associations motivate weather-aware evaluation; they do not identify this athlete's causal correction or calibrated interval. No quantitative transfer coefficient used. |
| [Liljegren et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18668404/) | Physical heat/mass-transfer natural wet-bulb and globe submodels; reported comparison against instrument WBGT better than 1°C at evaluated depots. | Requires appropriate radiation/airflow inputs and implementation validation. No simplified T/RH expression is called measured WBGT here; WBGT stays unavailable. Site validation does not establish athlete performance prediction. |
| [Racinais et al., 2015 heat consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26069301/) | Synthesis of training/competition heat evidence and acclimation practice; not a single individualized race-time trial. | Acclimation, duration, clothing, exposure and preparation matter. Does not supply a validated universal acclimation multiplier or sodium prescription; those remain outside this feature. |

Existing explicit running grade cost, cycling mechanics, ordered triathlon legs/transitions and selected swim pace remain scenario arithmetic. FTP does not imply CdA. Outdoor grid weather is not indoor HYROX climate. Unknown physiology, fatigue carryover, relative airflow, acclimation and currents do not become invented athlete-specific adjustments.
