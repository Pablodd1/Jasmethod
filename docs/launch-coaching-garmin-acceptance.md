# Launch acceptance: daily coaching and Garmin Connect

Run on the exact release with a consenting athlete. Start with one account and keep the evidence private.

1. Review an existing seven-day cycle against confirmed availability, recent tolerated load, goals and race priority. Record original daily minutes and session purposes. Verify an unscheduled activity is logged separately and the planned workout remains visible.
2. Save a planned result and an unplanned result. Check the next workout before and after the relevant check-in. A missing or restricted record must not be treated as zero load. Verify any change shows the original and reviewed duration. If the plan is unchanged, the review must not claim that a change was made.
3. Ask Koach about the workout and race plan. Compare the private cards to the saved records. Confirm English and Spanish, text and actual microphone dictation. Confirm device-derived records stay out of external AI context.
4. Download a supported structured FIT workout. On the intended Garmin model, transfer it and inspect sport, steps, targets and alerts. A downloaded file alone is not device evidence.
5. For direct Garmin Connect, record the approved app, exact granted permissions and active production gates. Verify a new activity arrives once and is linked to the correct athlete. Test workout creation and calendar read-back separately. Then confirm the workout on the watch after sync. Record the result of revision and cancellation. Do not mark an OAuth grant or Connect calendar read-back as watch receipt.

Core app launch and Garmin pilot are separate decisions. Keep direct Garmin off for general rollout until permission, production configuration, supported push authentication and actual device acceptance are documented. Do not advertise all-sport or historical sync.
