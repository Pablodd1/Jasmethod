# Scoped correction to the historical mandatory-plyometrics rule

Date: 2026-10-08

The owner authorized cautious implementation with independent science and athlete-experience audits. This records an exception to the September historical wording that every generator must prescribe plyometrics. Performance evidence does not establish a universal dose, injury prevention, movement eligibility or tolerance for every athlete.

Scope: preserve existing scheduled strength/power slots, allocated duration, phase structure and daily rest/injury gates. Generic templates and regenerated alternatives use familiar coach-reviewed strength or movement. Jumps, depth jumps and Olympic lifting are optional only after review of movement technique, experience, equipment and current tolerance. No approval field is invented and no template claims review actually occurred. Without that information, no automatic explosive dose is supplied.

Structured generic strength steps no longer insert jumps. Alternative conditioning remains an option with a different stimulus; it is not advertised as equivalent to the original sport-specific session. Existing stored history is not rewritten. Explicit reviewed protocols remain governed by their existing eligibility contract.

Evidence correction: the legacy registry ID `ramirezcampillo2022` is retained to avoid breaking references. Its former citation was nonspecific. The runners review verified for the narrowed performance statement is Ramírez-Campillo et al. (2021), “Effects of jump training on physical fitness and athletic performance in endurance runners: A meta-analysis”, PMID 33956587, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33956587/. It does not validate JMM or establish injury-prevention efficacy for this product.

Acceptance: generic generation/regeneration and executable strength steps supply no compulsory jumps or Olympic lifts; slot allocation and time caps remain intact; low-zone and rest prescriptions do not gain power work. Hardware, human coaching approval and athlete outcome validation remain unobserved unless independently recorded.
